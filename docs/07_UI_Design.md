# 07. UI/UX Design & Style Guidelines

## 1. Visual Identity & Brand Philosophy
**Open Concept Bangla** is designed to inspire trust, accessibility, and clarity for Bengali citizens. 
- **Primary Brand Color**: Deep Forest Green (`#0d3b22` / `emerald-900`) representing institutional reliability and public service authority.
- **Secondary / Accent Colors**: Vibrant Leaf Green (`#16a34a` / `emerald-600`) and Warm Amber (`#f59e0b` / `amber-500`) for badges, highlights, and calls to action.
- **Backgrounds**: Slate White (`#f8fafc` / `slate-50`) and Card White (`#ffffff`) with subtle border lines (`#e2e8f0`).
- **Dark Mode Compatibility**: Slate-900 surface with emerald accents.

---

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Bengali Font**: `Hind Siliguri` (clean, modern Bengali sans-serif with high readability on low-resolution displays).
- **Secondary English Font**: `Inter` (for numerals, system status codes, and administrative tables).
- **Hierarchy**:
  - Main Hero Banner: 32px – 48px Bold
  - Section Titles: 24px – 28px SemiBold with left green accent bar
  - Card Headings: 18px – 20px Medium
  - Body Text: 15px – 16px Regular with 1.6 line height for comfortable reading

---

## 3. UI Component Specifications
1. **Top Notice Bar**: Breaking alerts (urgent scholarship dates or scheme notifications) with marquee / slider effect.
2. **Main Navigation**: Fixed/sticky header with logo, primary navigation links, integrated search trigger, and user auth buttons.
3. **Service Grid**: Responsive 4-column card grid with colored SVG icon containers, Bengali category title, and quick link badge.
4. **Tools Card**: Prominent interactive cards showing target resolution, privacy badge ("১০০% নিরাপদ - কোনো ফাইল আপলোড হয় না"), and direct tool launch.
5. **Blog Card**: 16:9 featured image thumbnail, category pill, publication date, view counter, Bengali title, and excerpt.
